# QA Checklist — 30 sec per lead (before send)

OpenClaw flags pain → YOU verify:

1. **Booking/Ordering** — Does WhatsApp/phone/3rd party count? Don't claim "no booking" if WhatsApp is there. Check header, footer, Instagram bio.
2. **Mobile** — Open on phone: is CTA thumb-reachable? Is phone number clickable?
3. **Speed/Trust** — Does site load <3s? Are Google reviews visible? Arabic toggle?

**If AI is wrong, rewrite line 1:** e.g., "Saw you take WhatsApp bookings — we can make it one-tap + auto-confirm."

**Badge honesty:** All demos must show "Concept Demo — Not a real client" — no fake testimonials.
